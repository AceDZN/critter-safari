# Critter Safari —50-level reading campaign

The ordered campaign is open to every player. There is no grade picker, age gate or grade-dependent level limit. Difficulty develops through50levels/63actual rescue objectives: preference clauses, independent size/speed comparisons, negation, compound groups, bounded extremes, equality/not-as, described references, rescued-animal references, exclusions/conditional exceptions and combined2–3-rescue briefs. Source grades are internal provenance, not an assessment or access rule.

These are fictional individual preferences, habits, sizes and usual unbaited speeds. Species never determines a preference or a speed rank. Lures/calming alter motion, not the original measurement used for English truth. Reading/help and held-tool planning pause without penalty.

Every objective requires a real physical capture; wrong animals remain capturable and releasable. Later rescues remove an actor from the candidate pool while retaining its original facts in the visible roster. A previous-rescue reference resolves only against genuine successful rescue records in the current level. Completion requires every objective. A direct lesson link to50 does not create49earlier successes.

The game practices reading and using English evidence. It does not demonstrate productive grammar, speaking, validated learning gains, complete grade coverage or Ministry endorsement. The sentences below are original game fixtures, not copied Workbook text. The original five-contract curriculum remains in docs/campaign/legacy-curriculum.md as historical evidence.
