# Visual review

## Targets inspected before implementation

Built-in image_gen produced two separate 1672×941 concept views. The lead inspected target1 before gameplay source, and target2 before renderer work. Exact prompts and generation mode are saved under docs/visual-targets. Target1 establishes low first-person view, sunlit broad leaves, clear room to circle, bubble trap by twig, airborne lure and three natural creature silhouettes. Target2 establishes a captured puffling, escaping bait carrier and recoverable reset opportunity. Neither is a real capture.

## Current known differences

The authored environment uses a sunlit Three.js garden, broad leaf silhouettes, moss, stones, twig, dew, puddle, brass/wood gadget and restrained parchment contract. Required Tripo creatures, rigs and clips remain absent due to a verified browser-control timeout. Explicitly labeled diagnostic creature geometry is present only to exercise mechanics. It cannot meet the target's creature silhouette/material/animation fidelity. The net icon introduced by target2 is outside the authored two-tool scope; actual tools remain bait and reusable bubble trap.

Actual same-viewport captures, side-by-side comparison and any subsequent fixes will be appended after browser rendering succeeds. No final target match is claimed.
