## שאלות ותשובות

### מהו An extreme needs a set?

Among these three routes, Hill Field has the longest main route: 1,100 metres. Willow Garden has the shortest: 500 metres. The phrase among these three routes limits the claim to the supplied set. The guide cannot prove that Hill Field has the longest route in the region, because it gives no information about every other route there. A superlative expresses a relationship, not an unlimited label attached to a place.

### מהו Building a superlative phrase?

Short adjectives commonly use -est: the longest route, the shortest walk. With many longer adjectives, use most: the most convenient meeting area. Some forms change irregularly, such as good and best. The names the particular extreme in these examples: 'the longest of the three'. It is not required before every possible superlative construction. A possessive can fill that role: 'my best drawing', not 'my the best drawing'. Compare two places with longer than; identify an extreme within a stated group with the longest.

### מהו Highest on a measure is not best for everyone?

The longest route may suit a visitor who wants more walking and be unsuitable for someone with limited time. A large open meeting space may suit one event while a roof matters more for another. The recommendation combines a stated need with two relevant features. It also preserves a limitation about steps. 'The best place' without those conditions would hide the reasoning. A different group may reasonably make a different choice. A useful judgement explains both the comparison and the reason it matters.

### Three fictional places have two, four, and three benches. Is it accurate to say that the place with four benches is the most comfortable place for every visitor?

The place with four benches has the most benches among the three fictional places. That count does not prove it is the most comfortable for every visitor. Comfort depends on other features and individual needs, so the comparison must keep its stated measure.

## Why does a superlative need a comparison set?

A superlative places something at an extreme within a comparison set. The longest of three routes is not automatically the longest route in a whole country. The set matters. The visit guide and observations below are invented examples, not real travel advice.

צורת הפלגה כמו ״הארוך ביותר״ משווה בתוך קבוצה. הארוך מבין שלושה מסלולים אינו בהכרח הארוך במדינה כולה; תמיד בודקים מהי קבוצת ההשוואה.

## Three Places for a School Visit

Willow Garden: The main route is 500 metres long. There are two benches and a sheltered meeting area. A small group can sit together beside the entrance. The written guide describes a smooth main surface and four steps near the far gate. River Corner: The main route is 800 metres long. There are four benches and no steps on the described route. Part of the surface is rough. The meeting area has a roof, but it is smaller than Willow Garden's meeting area. Hill Field: The main route is 1,100 metres long. There are three benches and a large open meeting space. There is no roof over that space. The route includes a slope and two sets of steps. The guide gives no measurements for the slope.

The phrase among these three routes limits the claim to the supplied set. The guide cannot prove that Hill Field has the longest route in the region, because it gives no information about every other route there.

## Language for a bounded judgement

comparison set / extreme

A comparison set is the group being considered. An extreme is the highest, lowest, or furthest end of a quality within that group.

sheltered / convenient

Sheltered means protected by a covering or barrier in this context. Convenient means useful or easy for a particular purpose. A covered meeting area can be convenient for a group needing a place for papers.

claim / support

A claim is a statement presented for consideration. Its support is the relevant information behind it. A heading alone may make a claim without supplying the needed support.

עזרה בעברית

קבוצת ההשוואה מגדירה מה נכלל בבדיקה. ״הנוח ביותר למטרה״ דורש להסביר את המטרה, ולטענה צריך להיות בסיס מעבר לכותרת שלה.

## The guide's figures in one place

Main route lengths: Willow Garden 500 metres; River Corner 800 metres; Hill Field 1,100 metres. Benches: Willow Garden two; River Corner four; Hill Field three. Covered meeting area: Willow Garden yes; River Corner yes; Hill Field no. These fictional figures make different comparisons possible. River Corner has the most benches of the three, but Hill Field has the longest route. One place does not automatically rank highest on every feature.

## A supported claim and an overreach

A claim the table supports

'River Corner has the most benches of these three places.' All three counts are supplied, so the relevant set is complete.

A claim beyond the table

'River Corner has the most benches of every park in the country.' The table contains only three places and cannot support that national comparison.

עזרה בעברית

הנתונים על שלושה מקומות מאפשרים מסקנה על שלושתם. טענה על כל הפארקים במדינה מרחיבה את קבוצת ההשוואה מעבר למה שהמקור בדק.

## A recommendation for one group

Our fictional group needs a short walk and a covered area large enough to discuss drawings. Willow Garden is the most convenient of these three choices for those needs: its route is the shortest, and its sheltered area fits the group. The steps near the far gate still matter. A visitor who cannot use steps needs more route information before choosing. River Corner has more benches, but bench count is not this group's main priority.

## Keep the comparison fair

Check the quality, the comparison set, and the reader's priorities. A number can identify an extreme for one feature. A recommendation needs the further explanation of why that feature helps this reader.

עזרה בעברית

לפני שימוש ב״הכי״ בדקו שלושה דברים: מה המאפיין, אילו אפשרויות נכללות בקבוצה ולמה המאפיין חשוב לקורא. השיא לבדו אינו מחליף המלצה מנומקת.
