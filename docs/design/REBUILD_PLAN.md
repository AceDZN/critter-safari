# Critter Safari rebuild — 26 October 2026

## Human feedback and new gate

The human found the diagnostic critters weird and the first-person gameflow confusing, difficult to control and boring. They explicitly requested planning and a rebuild, with fresh mockup screenshots before new gameplay implementation. Existing concept targets are historical; they do not approve this redesign. Music already authored in the preceding work can be retained. No new gameplay implementation begins until fresh built-in imagegen views are generated and inspected.

## New promise

A bright tabletop woodland toy: tempt the right animal into a bubble catcher and enjoy the chain reaction. The entire small clearing is visible from a fixed elevated three-quarter camera. Recognizable, appealing rabbit/tortoise/small-deer silhouettes replace diagnostic fantasy lumps, with coherent real acquired 3D assets and useful skeletal animations.

## Two direct gestures

1. Drag the visible catcher from the tray onto grass. A grounded ring shows its actual capture area; valid ground is broadly readable without a correct-target beacon. The catcher can be moved with the same gesture.
2. Drag one of two visible food tokens—leaf or berry—onto the ground. It lands with a short arc. Nearby animals that prefer that food notice, sniff and approach. The catcher seals automatically around the first animal entering its ring.

No walking keys, first-person camera, crosshair, range aiming, hidden tool mode or separate pulse timing. English determines whom to catch; spatial choices and animal behavior determine what actually gets caught. No tap-creature answer buttons, target snapping or automatic bait choice.

## First thirty seconds

Open on two similar animals and a short English diet clue. No title wall or required spreadsheet. One Hebrew instruction prompts catcher placement, then changes to food placement. Clear anticipation, a responsive approach, a bubble pop and a short musical reward establish the loop. Wrong catches remain visible and recover with one release button; correct catches earn a small journal stamp and an obvious next-contract button. Each action has a clear visible next step.

## Fun and escalation

Competing feeders can steal bait or reach the catcher first. A second food drop can distract a rival using the same gesture. Small rocks change approach routes. Moving the catcher and testing a new lure position is quick and forgiving. Do not add another interaction rule merely to increase difficulty.

1. Two similar animals; one diet/preference clause; generous placement.
2. Three candidates; size and usual speed deliberately differ.
3. Two constraints; an obstacle and a same-food competitor.
4. Same scene as3, different English target from a bounded superlative.
5. New names, trait assignments and terrain; same two gestures; no solution hint.

Exact fixtures and independent oracles were reauthored after the visual gate and pass the current test suite. Comparison truth remains based on usual unbaited speed on the same surface. Field notes/translation pause the world and remain optional before the first action. Do not make a target the only capturable creature or assign a fixed correct color.

## Visual direction and mockups

Achievable polished stylized WebGL, not cinematic photorealism. Sunlit earth, fine grassy edges and open space, restrained foliage around the perimeter, consistent animal scale and expressive faces. The world dominates; no dark canopy, giant gadget or huge labels. A small cream LTR English contract, a short Hebrew current-action prompt, a tangible bottom tool tray and five journal stamps are sufficient. Fresh views: first useful trap placement; later capture with a rival and release/retry action. Keep targets and actual captures separate.

## Acceptance before calling the rebuild successful

Observe a new-player-equivalent normal-input run: useful first action within ten seconds, a clear next action without coaching, actual lure/capture, meaningful wrong-catch recovery and all five contracts including transfer. Touch uses the same direct gestures. Validate keyboard accessibility without requiring keyboard memorization. Real models, rigs and clips are an explicit gate, not an assumed quality claim. Compare actual views against both fresh targets and fix material differences.
